About

Attendo is developed and operated by Samundar Singh, Kota, Rajasthan, India (sachoratechnologies@gmail.com). It is an attendance, field-staff and payroll app: companies (employers) use it to manage their staff; employees use it to punch in / out, record trips, apply for leave and see salary slips.

Your employer decides how attendance and payroll data of its staff is used. Attendo processes this data for the employer.

1.Information we collect

  • Account: name, email, role. Passwords are stored only in encrypted form.
  • Company: name, code, owner and (optional) address, phone, email, website, GST, PAN, TAN, licence and registration numbers.
  • Attendance: punch-in / out time, a live camera selfie and location (GPS and address).
  • Work records: office & shift, trips (places, locations, distance), service call orders (customer details entered by employer, remark, selfie, location).
  • Requests & messages: leave requests, salary details entered by the employer, company notices, and feedback you send.

We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS or other files. No advertising. We never sell personal data.

2.Permissions

  • Camera: selfie for punch-in / out and service calls (camera only, no gallery).
  • Location: where you punch in / out, attend a call or run a trip. During an active trip location is used in the background with a notification and stops when the trip stops.
  • Notifications: "Trip in progress" notification.
  • Fingerprint / face / PIN: checked only by your phone. Attendo never receives your fingerprint or face data.

3.Offline use & data on your phone

  • Without internet the app shows the last saved data and keeps your actions (like punch-in) on the phone, sending them automatically when internet returns.
  • Offline, the location used is the phone's last known location, or "nil".
  • The full attendance photo is saved in the app folder on your phone (Attendo Photos); a small compressed copy is uploaded for your employer.
  • Saved screen data is cleared on logout; uninstalling removes all app data from the phone.

4.Who can see your data

  • Employees see only their own records, plus their company's notices and profile.
  • Employers / approved managers see records of their own company's staff.
  • Other companies cannot see your data (enforced on the server).
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, login, photo storage), Brevo (account emails), Android / Google location & address services, Google Maps (when you open a map link).
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

5.How long we keep data

Data is kept while the account or company is active so the company has its attendance and salary history. Deleted data is removed from backups in the normal backup cycle.

6.Delete your account

Settings → Delete Account. An employee / manager account deletes your own records. An employer account deletes the whole company with all its staff accounts and records. This cannot be undone.

7.Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), no plain-text passwords, server-side access rules, and optional fingerprint / PIN app lock with auto-lock.

8.Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data and withdraw permissions at any time, including rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9.Children

Attendo is a workplace app and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Salary & payroll data

  • Your employer enters salary details: basic or per-day wage, number of days, HRA, allowances, bonus and deductions (PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, advance, other).
  • The app uses your attendance days and approved leave to calculate absent-day (LOP) deduction and net pay.
  • Only you and your employer / approved managers can see your salary slips.

Company details, notices & feedback

  • Employers may add company address, phone, email, website, GST, PAN, TAN, licence and registration numbers. These are business details shown to the company's own staff.
  • Notices sent by your employer are visible to all staff of that company.
  • Feedback you send is read by the Attendo team and may also be visible to your employer / managers.

Device security setting

Fingerprint / PIN app lock and auto-lock time are saved on this phone and apply to the account logged in on it. Logging out does not turn them off; Delete Account resets them.
